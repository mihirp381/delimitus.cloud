Privacy

Effective 30 October 2026

This page says what personal data Delimitus collects through delimitus.com and the Delimitus console, why, who else handles it and how long we keep it.

Who we are

Delimitus runs delimitus.com and the Delimitus console, where coding agents deploy company software. For any question about your data, or to ask us to see, correct or delete it, write to privacy@delimitus.com.

The website

delimitus.com sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other sites.

If you request a pilot, the form sends us your first name, last name, work email, company and, if you fill it in, the tools you use. Each request is stored in a private storage bucket in our Google Cloud project. We keep it for 12 months to answer you and use it only for that; after 12 months it is deleted. Only the founder reads them.

To slow abuse, the form counts requests from each network address for one minute, in memory, and does not store the address. The site writes no access log of its own. Google Cloud, which hosts it, keeps a request log of each visit (the time, the network address, the browser and the page asked for) for 30 days, which we use only to run and protect the site.

The console and sign-in

The console (console.delimitus.com), its sign-in (auth.delimitus.com) and its API (api.delimitus.com) are for people whose organisation uses Delimitus. You sign in through WorkOS, our sign-in provider, with Google, Microsoft or the identity provider your organisation uses, such as Okta. Your organisation may also connect its directory, so that people it adds or removes there are added or removed here.

When you sign in, we receive your name, your email address, whether your provider has verified it, and your provider's id for you. We keep your name, email address and that id, with your role in your organisation and, where your organisation connects its directory, the names of the groups you belong to. We do not keep a profile picture.

Delimitus sets cookies only on auth.delimitus.com and on the hosts of your organisation's apps: short-lived ones (10 minutes or less) during sign-in or while an app starts, and a session cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 12 hours. Your organisation's apps may set their own cookies; WorkOS and your identity provider set theirs on their own sites. The console keeps two settings in your browser: your choice of light or dark theme, and the last organisation id or invite code you entered, so the sign-in page can offer it again.

We use this data to sign you in; to show who you are to you and to the people in your organisation; to apply the access your organisation's admins grant; to record who did what in your organisation's audit log; and to send service emails, such as a welcome to your organisation's first admin, approval requests, reminders and decisions. When you open one of your organisation's apps, we pass that app your name and email address so it knows who you are. We count how the console is used under a pseudonym, never your name or email address. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

Google user data

If you sign in with Google, we ask Google, through WorkOS, only for the basic profile it shares at sign-in: your name, email address and, if you have one, profile picture. We ask for no other Google data, such as your mail, files or calendar.

We use data obtained through Google sign-in only to provide the console's features described above: signing you in, showing who you are to your organisation, passing your name and email address to the organisation's apps you open, and sending service emails. We share it only with your organisation, its apps and the processors below. Delimitus's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who else handles your data

  • Google Cloud hosts the website, the console and their data, in its us-central1 region in the United States.
  • WorkOS runs sign-in and, where your organisation connects one, its directory. It receives what your identity provider sends about you.
  • Our email provider delivers the service emails described above.

We share personal data with no one else, unless the law requires us to.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

  • Pilot requests are deleted after 12 months, or sooner if you ask.
  • Server logs are kept for 30 days.
  • Your console account is kept while your organisation uses Delimitus. When your organisation removes you, in its directory or in the console, your sign-in ends and your account is deactivated. On request, we overwrite your name and email address and delete the link to your sign-in account.
  • The audit log is append-only: our service cannot change or delete its entries. It records who acted by an internal id and today stores no names, email addresses or network addresses.
  • Usage counts are kept under the pseudonym; on request, we delete yours.

To ask for any of this, write to privacy@delimitus.com. If you use the console through your employer, you can also ask your organisation's admins.

Changes to this page

When what we collect or how we use it changes, we update this page and its effective date.
